Coping Mechanisms

Choose a small action that feels manageable right now.

🌬️

Slow breathing

Breathe in gently for 4 counts and out for 6 counts. Repeat for a minute without forcing your breath.

🚶

Move your body

Take a short walk, stretch, or do light movement to release tension.

📝

Write it down

Note what is worrying you and identify one small action you can take.

🤝

Connect

Talk with a trusted friend, family member, teacher, or support person.

🌙

Rest & routine

Keep a regular sleep schedule and take short breaks between tasks.

🧑‍⚕️

Seek support

If stress is persistent or affecting daily life, consider a qualified mental-health professional.

About StressCare AI

StressCare AI is a mini-project prototype focused on stress awareness and healthy coping mechanisms. It aims to provide a simple self-reflection check-in, general wellness suggestions, and a supportive chatbot interface.

Technology Domains

Web Development

Responsive interface, forms, page navigation, and client-side interactions.

Full Stack Development

Designed to extend with APIs and a backend service.

DBMS

Designed to extend with secure storage of accounts and check-in history.

Cloud Computing

Ready for deployment to a hosting provider to share a public URL.

Note: This current downloadable version is a frontend demo. Real account authentication, database storage, cloud deployment, and AI API connection need separate setup.
